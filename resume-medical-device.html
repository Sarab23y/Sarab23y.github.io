Research Experience

Graduate Research Assistant — Computational Cardiac Mechanics

University of Kentucky · Spring 2024–Present

  • Develop and extend a coupled left-ventricular simulation framework integrating passive myocardial mechanics, active contraction, half-sarcomere kinetics, and cardiovascular circulation.
  • Compared nonlinear anisotropic myocardial constitutive formulations to determine how material-law choice changes ventricular deformation, stress, pressure-volume behavior, and remodeling predictions.
  • Developed material-validation workflows using biaxial, shear, and passive-inflation simulations to assess soft-tissue constitutive behavior before organ-level studies.
  • Investigated myocardial fiber disarray and quantified resulting changes in pump function, mechanical efficiency, ATP-related energetics, and baroreflex compensation.
  • Currently developing an agent-based fibrosis model coupled with finite-element cardiac mechanics to study how spatial fibrosis alters myocardial mechanics and ventricular function.
  • Analyze pressure-volume loops, stress/strain distributions, fiber architecture, and spatial simulation outputs using Python, ParaView, and scientific post-processing workflows.

Engineering Data & Machine Learning

  • Applied vision-language models and regression methods to electrospinning video data for prediction of fiber morphology.
  • Built repeatability and cross-validation workflows to evaluate robustness of model-derived features and predictions.
