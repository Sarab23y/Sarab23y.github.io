Research

Multiscale and computational approaches to cardiovascular mechanics.

Passive Myocardial Constitutive Modeling

I investigate how nonlinear anisotropic passive constitutive laws influence ventricular stress, deformation, pressure-volume behavior, and predicted growth and remodeling, including comparisons of Guccione- and Holzapfel-Ogden-based formulations.

Nonlinear FEAConstitutive LawsSoft TissuePV Loops

Fiber Disarray, Efficiency & Baroreflex

I developed computational studies of myocardial fiber disarray to quantify how progressive disruption of fiber architecture affects ventricular mechanics, mechanical efficiency, ATP-related energetics, and baroreflex compensation.

Fiber ArchitectureEnergeticsBaroreflex

Agent-Based Fibrosis Modeling

My current work develops an agent-based fibrosis model coupled with finite-element cardiac mechanics to study how spatially evolving fibrosis alters tissue mechanics and ventricular function.

Agent-Based ModelingFibrosisMultiscale Modeling

Scientific ML for Electrospinning

I use pretrained and fine-tuned vision-language models to extract video-level representations from electrospinning experiments and evaluate embedding repeatability, bottleneck representations, feature variability, and regression performance for fiber-diameter prediction.

VLMsEmbeddingsRegression

MyoFE_H

MyoFE_H is my active research codebase for coupled left-ventricular mechanics. It integrates finite-element deformation, passive myocardial material behavior, half-sarcomere kinetics, active contraction, lumped circulation, remodeling modules, MPI-based execution, and scientific post-processing.
