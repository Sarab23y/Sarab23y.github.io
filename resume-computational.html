Research Experience

Graduate Research Assistant — Computational Cardiac Mechanics

University of Kentucky · Spring 2024–Present

  • Develop and extend MyoFE_H, a multiphysics finite-element framework coupling nonlinear left-ventricular mechanics, spatially distributed half-sarcomere kinetics, active contraction, and lumped-parameter circulation.
  • Implement and evaluate anisotropic passive myocardial constitutive laws, including Guccione- and Holzapfel-Ogden-based formulations, to quantify effects on stress, pressure-volume behavior, deformation, and growth/remodeling predictions.
  • Developed computational studies of myocardial fiber disarray and quantified its effects on ventricular mechanics, mechanical efficiency, energetics, and baroreflex compensation.
  • Currently developing an agent-based fibrosis model coupled with finite-element cardiac mechanics to study spatially evolving fibrosis and tissue-level mechanical adaptation.
  • Run distributed simulations using FEniCS/DOLFIN, Python, MPI, PETSc, SLURM, and Linux HPC environments; perform nonlinear Newton solves and quadrature-point calculations.
  • Develop validation and post-processing workflows for biaxial/shear material tests, passive ventricular inflation, stress/strain fields, pressure-volume loops, energetics, and ParaView visualization.

Machine Learning for Engineering Data

  • Applied pretrained and fine-tuned vision-language models to electrospinning videos to extract high-dimensional representations for fiber-diameter prediction.
  • Evaluated embedding repeatability, bottleneck representations, feature variability, regression performance, and repeated cross-validation.
