# Sara Bakhtiari — Research Website

Personal research website for **Sara Bakhtiari**, PhD Candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Kentucky.

Live site: https://sarab23y.github.io

Research areas include computational cardiac mechanics, nonlinear finite-element modeling, passive myocardial constitutive laws, myocardial fiber disarray, mechanical efficiency, baroreflex compensation, growth/remodeling, fibrosis, high-performance scientific computing, and scientific machine learning.

Main research code: https://github.com/Sarab23y/MyoFE_H
