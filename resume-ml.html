Research Experience

Graduate Research Assistant — Computational Cardiac Mechanics

University of Kentucky · Spring 2024–Present

  • Develop large-scale finite-element cardiac simulations in Python/FEniCS that couple mechanics, active contraction, circulation, and spatial biological state variables.
  • Design reproducible HPC workflows using MPI, PETSc, SLURM, structured simulation outputs, and automated post-processing for high-dimensional spatial/time-series data.
  • Developed computational studies of constitutive uncertainty, fiber disarray, mechanical efficiency, and physiological compensation, integrating model outputs across local and organ scales.
  • Currently coupling an agent-based fibrosis model with finite-element mechanics, creating a multiscale computational framework linking spatial biological evolution to tissue mechanics.

Vision-Language Models & Machine Learning for Electrospinning

  • Applied pretrained and fine-tuned vision-language models to electrospinning videos and extracted high-dimensional hidden-state embeddings for downstream prediction.
  • Developed learned low-dimensional bottleneck representations and compared them with full embeddings and randomly selected features.
  • Designed repeated embedding-generation, feature-selection, and cross-validation studies to quantify representation variability and predictive robustness.
  • Built regression pipelines for predicting electrospun fiber diameter from video-derived representations using Python and scikit-learn.
